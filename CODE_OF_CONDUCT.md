# Code of Conduct

Maieusis is a scientific and engineering collaboration. We want participation
to be safe, constructive, and open to people across fields and levels of
experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal attributes.
- State uncertainty and distinguish observation, inference, and opinion.
- Give specific, actionable review feedback.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, authorship, data rights, and embargoes.
- Welcome questions from people who are new to coding agents, model APIs, or a
  scientific domain.
- Stop and correct behavior that could expose credentials, private data, or
  restricted source material.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
misrepresentation, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review spaces,
and project-organized events or communications. Report conduct concerns to
`dracoxu@uchicago.edu`. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
Project maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or
take other proportionate action to protect the community.
