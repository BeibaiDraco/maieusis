# Decision-related population structure after richer movement accounting

This is a development planning dossier. It is planning-only and does not report scientific results.
Dataset grounding level: `sample_inspected`.
Dataset claim status: `unverified`.

## Question Family

A family testing whether apparent decision-related activity survives richer embodied alternatives or is better understood as behaviorally structured activity, with target-preservation and reinterpretation variants.

## Scientific Motivation

Population activity associated with decisions may encode a latent decision construct, richer movement and posture, or inseparable mixtures; coarse motor controls may leave this ambiguity unresolved.

## Dataset Leverage

Dataset claim status is unverified; observed-depth label is sample_inspected. Variant 2: If concurrent pose, movement, neural, choice, response-time, event, and state-related measurements are sufficiently synchronized and complete, they may support nested held-out comparisons of rich behavioral features against event timing, locomotion, arousal, and latent-state summaries, with pre-response and execution-independent outcome prediction assessed separately from neural prediction. ## Competing Explanations And Controls

Competing explanations that must remain visible, by variant: Variant 1: Any incremental neural prediction reflects unmeasured, poorly measured, or temporally misaligned movement, posture, arousal, or other internal state rather than a choice-related component distinct from behavior. The behavioral model absorbs genuine choice-related information because incipient movement is downstream of the decision and therefore shares its predictive structure. Apparent incremental prediction results from unequal model flexibility, temporal leakage, or session-specific task and sensorimotor contingencies. A reproducible residual is choice-associated but reflects feedback, common input, or another noncausal correlate rather than a neural representation that causes the decision. Variant 2: Rich features improve neural prediction because they describe overt or uninstructed movements, without carrying decision-relevant information. Apparent choice or response-time prediction is produced by alignment with task events or leakage from overt response execution. High-dimensional pose is a flexible proxy for arousal or another lower-dimensional latent behavioral state rather than distinct embodied structure. Any advantage arises from unequal model flexibility, temporal autocorrelation, or measurement quality rather than the content of the rich behavioral representation.
