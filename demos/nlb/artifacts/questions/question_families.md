# Question family — Context-dependent balance of autonomous and input-linked motor dynamics

- Shared scientific tension: Low-dimensional reach dynamics can be consistent with internally organized evolution, but trajectory constraints and behavioral feedback may also shape the observed activity. Straight and curved reaches offer a proposal-stage contrast without making either account uniquely identifiable from geometry alone.
- Summary: Tests whether delayed-reach population dynamics retain a common autonomous-like organization or become more input-linked when trajectory demands change, while keeping preparatory and execution-period interpretations separate.
- Evidence basis: **abstract-only** — this family's supporting literature is not fulltext-verified (full text was not available to this system).

## Variants

- **Preparation-focused test of shared versus path-specific organization** — During movement preparation, does population-state organization generalize between straight and curved reaches, or does curvature demand produce distinct preparatory organization?
  - distinct from siblings: This variant concerns prospective organization before movement and interprets condition dependence as path-specific preparation, not feedback-linked execution.
- **Execution-focused test of autonomous-like versus input-linked dynamics** — During reach execution, are curved-path population dynamics disproportionately associated with ongoing behavioral input relative to straight-path dynamics?
  - distinct from siblings: This variant targets execution-period associations with ongoing behavior and interprets curvature effects through the autonomy-input tension rather than preparatory-state specificity.

Richer covariates can adjudicate these accounts without being treated merely as nuisance.
- Summary: Tests whether apparent neural organization for curved reaching reflects trajectory-level structure beyond simple kinematics, while separating prospective geometry from execution-related residual structure.
- Evidence basis: **abstract-only** — this family's supporting literature is not fulltext-verified (full text was not available to this system).

## Variants
