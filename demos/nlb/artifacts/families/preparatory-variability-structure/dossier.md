# Functional structure of preparatory population variability

This is a development planning dossier. It is planning-only and does not report scientific results.
Dataset grounding level: `schema_metadata_inspected`.
Dataset claim status: `unverified`.

## Question Family

Reframes preparatory variability from a scalar decline into geometric questions about alignment with upcoming movement or association with trial-to-trial behavioral performance.

## Scientific Motivation

Preparatory variability may be generic noise that contracts before movement, or its orientation may contain structured information about upcoming trajectories and behavioral readiness.

## Dataset Leverage

Dataset claim status is unverified; observed-depth label is schema_metadata_inspected. Dataset leverage remains a planning hypothesis, by variant: Variant 1: Delayed reaching and trajectory measurements may allow a later planner to define candidate prospective signal directions independently of preparatory variability. Variant 2: If delayed-reaching data jointly contain preparatory population activity, instructed path information, trajectories, and temporally aligned behavioral-state measurements, they may support a later test of whether a distinct preparatory-instability measure predicts trajectory quality beyond measured alternatives. ## Competing Explanations And Controls

Competing explanations that must remain visible, by variant: Variant 1: Selective contraction along upcoming-trajectory dimensions could reflect functional stabilization of a planned movement.
