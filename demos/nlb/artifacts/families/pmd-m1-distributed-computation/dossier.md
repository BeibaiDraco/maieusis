# Localized versus distributed organization of reach planning and execution

This is a development planning dossier. It is planning-only and does not report scientific results.
Dataset grounding level: `sample_inspected`.
Dataset claim status: `unverified`.

## Question Family

Examines whether PMd/M1 distinctions are best understood through trajectory-demand sensitivity or through temporal transformation from preparation to execution.

## Scientific Motivation

PMd and M1 may make distinguishable contributions to reaching, but observed regional differences could reflect distributed computation, temporal staging, trajectory complexity, or measurement sensitivity rather than strict localization.

## Variant Plans

- Variant 1 (Spatial-scope test of trajectory-complexity structure): Is population structure associated with curved-path demand concentrated in PMd, concentrated in M1, or distributed across both regions?
  - Distinguishing test: This variant asks where curved-path-specific structure is expressed; it does not ask how regional organization changes from preparation to execution. The discriminating observation is A standardized comparison of curved-versus-straight population structure across both regions, interpreted relative to region-specific reliability, would distinguish concentrated and distributed accounts.
  - Planned analysis: Accepted with a complete curvature-isolated, reliability-standardized, equivalence- based plan honoring all three Owner rulings. Requires a new executor skill to implement region mapping with the M1 +96 correction, the continuous curvature index, the confound-partialled population encoding model, the reliability-equalization procedure, and the equivalence/precision decision rule. Claim ceiling is associational within a single subject and session; an INCONCLUSIVE outcome is possible given the 100-trial sample and must be reported honestly.
    - Refined question: Within a single-subject delayed reaching session, is curved-path-specific population structure (population variance uniquely attributable to trajectory curvature, after removing duration and other movement/target/distractor confounds) concentrated in PMd, concentrated in M1, or distributed comparably across both regions, judged on a reliability-standardized PMd-minus-M1 contrast with an affirmative equivalence rule?
    - Population and scope: Sorted-unit populations from one rhesus macaque (Jenkins), one session (2009-09-28), MC_Maze_Small train file: 72 PMd and 70 M1 units by the documented unit-ID convention, over 100 successful delayed center-out reaches (68 barriered curved, 32 no-barrier straight). Scope is within-subject and within-session; no across-subject or causal generalization is claimed.
    - Data sources: MC_Maze_Small train NWB (sub-Jenkins_ses-small_desc-train_behavior+ecephys.nwb): units table (spike_times, obs_intervals, heldout, electrodes), electrodes table (location M1/PMd), trials table (trial_version, num_barriers, maze_id, event times, num_targets, active_target, target_pos), and behavior processing module (hand_pos, hand_vel, cursor_pos, eye_pos) at 1 kHz.; Expected grain: One row per trial in the trials table (100 trials); per-unit spike-time series with per-trial observation intervals; 1 kHz continuous behavioral signals.; Required variables: units.id (region via leading digit), units.spike_times, units.obs_intervals, units.heldout, units.electrodes, electrodes.location, electrodes.group_name (region cross-check; M1 +96 row correction), trials.trial_version, trials.num_barriers, trials.maze_id, trials.start_time, trials.stop_time, trials.target_on_time, trials.go_cue_time, trials.move_onset_time, trials.rt, trials.delay, trials.num_targets, trials.active_target, trials.target_pos (target/distractor confounds), behavior.hand_pos, behavior.hand_vel (curvature index, speed profile, duration); Limitations: Planning-only inspection; establishes structure and coverage, not any neural effect. Behavioral curvature labels and hand_pos exist only in the train file; the test file has no behavior module and cannot extend the labeled sample (an internal audit identifier). Scope/coverage bound derived from the test file and dandiset metadata establishing the single-subject, single-session, 100-labeled-trial ceiling for this variant.; Expected grain: Dataset-level metadata and file-manifest facts.; Required variables: test file processing modules and trials columns; dandiset subject/session/count metadata; Limitations: Bounds the achievable sample and generalization; a real ceiling, not a repairable defect.
    - Unit of observation: One reach trial (movement epoch), with per-region population activity vectors.
    - Unit of inference: The trial is the resampling unit; the terminal inferential unit is the reliability-standardized PMd-minus-M1 curvature-unique structure contrast within this single subject/session. Inference is within-subject; the single session is the generalization ceiling.
    - Hierarchy and dependence: Trials are nested within 9 maze configurations and 3 versions and share a single subject/session. Curved and straight trials are pooled for the primary contrast with maze configuration and target direction handled as covariates or via condition-matching. Dependence is handled by trial-level cross-validation and bootstrap resampling that keep whole trials together; units are not treated as independent replicates for the regional claim (region reliability is standardized instead).
    - Analysis strategy: Assign region by the unit-ID leading digit (1=PMd, 2=M1); reconcile against electrodes.location after applying the documented M1 +96 electrode-row correction; keep any residual disagreement visible rather than dropping units (per DATASET_NOTES). Define the movement epoch per trial from move_onset_time to reach completion; time-normalize (warp) each trial's population activity and behavior to a common epoch so curved-path structure is defined independently of movement duration. Compute a continuous hand-path curvature index per trial (path-length / start-to-end displacement; maximum lateral deviation as a robustness variant) from hand_pos. Build a per-region population encoding/partialling model relating time-normalized population activity to the curvature index after accounting for non-curvature features: reach/endpoint direction (target_pos and hand endpoint angle), target configuration and distractor presence (num_targets, active_target), barrier presence (num_barriers), and kinematics (speed profile, peak speed, movement duration, reaction time). The curvature-unique population structure is the population variance/separability attributable to curvature net of these covariates. As a complementary discrete check, condition-match curved vs straight reaches on target direction and restrict to num_targets=1, then compute curved-vs-straight population separability so the label contrast is not confounded by direction or distractors. Reliability-standardize the PMd-M1 comparison: subsample each region to equal unit counts (min(72,70)) over many random draws; balanced cross-validated trial resampling; a bias-corrected / cross-validated separability estimator (cross-validated Mahalanobis distance or cross-validated decoder accuracy); express each region's curvature-unique structure relative to that region's own noise ceiling estimated from within-condition trial-to-trial reliability. Apply the prespecified decision rule on the standardized PMd-minus-M1 difference (see candidate_estimands and validation_strategy): CONCENTRATED, DISTRIBUTED, or INCONCLUSIVE.
    - Candidate estimands: Primary: standardized PMd-minus-M1 difference in reliability-normalized, curvature-unique population structure over the movement epoch, with a bootstrap precision interval. Per-region curvature-unique structure magnitude (relative to region noise ceiling) for PMd and for M1 separately. Equivalence outcome of the PMd-minus-M1 difference against a prespecified SESOI (two one-sided tests / interval-based).
    - Validation strategy: Prespecify a smallest regional difference of interest (SESOI) in standardized units before inspecting outcomes. Estimate the PMd-minus-M1 difference with a bootstrap/credible precision interval over unit-subsampling and trial resampling, and run an equivalence test (TOST / interval-based) against the SESOI. Decision: CONCENTRATED if the difference interval robustly excludes zero and exceeds the SESOI in one region; DISTRIBUTED only if the interval falls entirely within the equivalence bounds (affirmative comparability); INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. A non-significant contrast is never read as distributed. Report equivalence-test power given the 100-trial sample.
    - Split strategy: Trial-level (leakage-safe) cross-validation and bootstrap: whole trials assigned to train/test folds so no trial contributes to both fitting and evaluation of the separability metric; nested resampling for the unit-subsampling draws. No use of the benchmark test file (it lacks behavioral labels).
    - Diagnostics: Per-region unit counts, spike-count coverage over the movement epoch, and missingness of behavioral samples per trial. Distribution of the curvature index across versions/mazes and its collinearity with duration, speed, and target direction. Stability of the regional contrast across unit-subsampling draws and CV folds; noise-ceiling estimates per region.
    - Negative controls: Shuffle the curvature label across trials within matched condition strata; curvature-unique structure should collapse to chance. A scientifically irrelevant label (e.g., trial index parity or eye-position-only feature) should show no region-specific curvature-attributable structure.
    - Positive controls: Target/endpoint direction should be decodable from both regions' population activity, confirming the population signal and metric are functioning before interpreting the curvature contrast.
    - Alternative explanations: Duration and general movement-magnitude differences between curved and straight reaches (addressed by time-warping and kinematic partialling). Visual/obstacle, target, and distractor differences carried by barrier/version condition (addressed by covariate partialling and condition-matching). Unequal region-specific measurement sensitivity producing apparent concentration (addressed by unit-count subsampling, bias-corrected metric, and per-region noise-ceiling normalization). Small-sample sampling variability across maze configurations (addressed by pooling, cross-validation, and bootstrap precision intervals).
    - Predicted result patterns: Concentration: one region's reliability-standardized curvature-unique structure robustly exceeds the other's beyond the SESOI, supporting predictive regional specialization for curved-path processing. Distributed: the standardized PMd-minus-M1 difference falls within the equivalence bounds, supporting distributed organization. Inconclusive: the difference interval is wider than both the SESOI and zero-exclusion thresholds, which given the sample size is a plausible honest outcome and would not be reported as either concentration or distribution.
    - Claim ceiling: associational
    - Interpretation limits: Single subject (Jenkins) and single session cap the claim at a within-subject, within-session association; no across-subject or causal generalization. Only 100 labeled trials (68 curved / 32 straight) limit power; a well-powered SESOI may be unreachable and INCONCLUSIVE is a legitimate result, not a failure of the plan. Region assignment depends on the documented unit-ID convention and the M1 +96 correction; any unresolved metadata disagreement is kept visible rather than resolved by dropping units. Planning evidence is not a scientific result; no effect has been estimated or sought in this planning step. The curvature-unique structure is a proxy for curved-path demand; residual curvature-kinematics collinearity is mitigated but not guaranteed eliminated.
    - Resource estimate: Bounded single-session analysis: 142 units x 100 trials at 1 kHz behavior. Compute dominated by unit-subsampling x cross-validation x bootstrap resampling of a population separability metric; feasible on a single workstation (order minutes to a few hours depending on resample counts). No full-dataset or multi-session scaling required.
    - Why this serves the question: The plan preserves the variant's protected phenomenon (where curved-path-specific structure is expressed across PMd and M1), its target contrast (condition-relative curved-vs-straight structure, not preparation-to-execution staging), and its outcome meanings (concentration = predictive regional specialization; distributed = affirmatively established comparability). All three Owner rulings were incorporated: curvature isolated from duration and condition confounds, reliability-standardized regional comparison, and an equivalence-based inference for the distributed outcome.
    - Required new skills: Executor skill implementing: region assignment via unit-ID convention with the M1 +96 electrode-row correction and metadata reconciliation; continuous hand-path curvature index and movement-epoch time-warping; confound-partialled per-region population encoding/separability model; reliability-equalization (unit-count subsampling, bias-corrected cross-validated metric, per-region noise-ceiling normalization); and the prespecified SESOI equivalence/precision decision rule.
    - Unresolved planning decisions: Numeric SESOI value (framework fixed, number to be prespecified before execution). Include vs exclude heldout=True units (default include-all with heldin-only sensitivity check). Primary bias-corrected separability estimator choice (cv-Mahalanobis vs cv-decoder), the other as sensitivity.
  - What would support or weaken it: A robust regional concentration would support a predictive regional specialization hypothesis for curved-path processing. Comparable target-specific structure across regions would favor a distributed organization. Uncertain or sensitivity-dependent regional differences would leave spatial organization unresolved.
