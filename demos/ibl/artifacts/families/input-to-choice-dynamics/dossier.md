# How input influence changes during a decision

This is a development planning dossier. It is planning-only and does not report scientific results.
Dataset grounding level: `schema_metadata_inspected`.
Dataset claim status: `unverified`.

## Question Family

Develops two distinct dynamical accounts of decision formation: a within-episode change in input sensitivity and a distributed transformation of task information across processing levels.

## Scientific Motivation

Changing neural trajectories may mark a genuine transition in how inputs influence decisions, or may reflect continuous input-driven evolution and distributed transformation without a discrete regime change.

## Competing Explanations And Controls

Competing explanations that must remain visible: A genuine dynamical regime change reduces the influence of subsequent input and stabilizes the emerging decision.
