## Family 001: Invariant and reorganized population geometry during decision-making

- Shortlist disposition: **Shortlisted for planning; this is not scientific approval**
- Proposal review status: `model_generated`
- Authority ceiling: `verified`

### Scientific background

Tests whether population geometry preserves task-relevant meaning across naturally varying recording and behavioral contexts, or whether apparent stability and reorganization reflect different functional organizations.

### Shared scientific tension

Population representations may preserve decision-relevant organization across contexts, reorganize while preserving equivalent meaning, or change because sensory, movement, and sampling conditions differ.

### Family structure

- Semantic axes: target_contrast; claim_level; population_scope; outcome_meaning

- Proposal-stage uncertainties: The relevant contexts and their comparability are not yet operationalized.; Population geometry may be sensitive to sampling and limited repeated observations.; Behavioral meaning may not be uniquely identifiable from observational associations.
- Dataset assumptions: The dataset may support coarse comparisons across task recordings and behavioral contexts.; Movement information may permit later evaluation of embodied alternatives.; No exact joint coverage or feasibility is assumed.

Does decision-relevant population geometry remain sufficiently conserved across subjects, sessions, and laboratories to support predictive generalization of stimulus and choice relationships?

#### Scientific tension and why it matters
